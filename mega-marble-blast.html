1Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Mega Marble Blast (the "Game," "we," "us," or "our"), published by Ramram Dev, handles information when you install and play it. By using the Game you agree to the practices described here.

2Information We Do Not Collect

The Game does not collect, request, or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Account credentials or social-media logins (there is no sign-in)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, calendar, or files on your device
  • Advertising identifiers or cross-app tracking data
  • Payment or financial information (the Game contains no in-app purchases)

3Information Stored on Your Device

To let you continue where you left off, the Game saves a small amount of data in the app's local storage on your device only. This includes:

  • Your best score and "cleared" status for each stage
  • Your preferences, such as music on/off and volume level

This information stays on your device, is not readable by us, and is not uploaded anywhere. It is removed when you clear the app's storage/data or uninstall the Game.

4Internet Access and Web Fonts

The Game is designed to be played offline. It declares the standard Android INTERNET permission because the app framework it is built on (Capacitor) includes it by default, and because the Game loads its display fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com / fonts.gstatic.com). When those fonts are requested, Google may receive your device's IP address and standard request information as part of serving them, as described in Google's Privacy Policy. The Game itself does not use this connection to send any personal information to the developer, and it operates no analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

5Distribution Through Google Play

The Game is distributed through the Google Play Store. When you download, install, or update the Game, Google collects and processes information under its own terms, independently of us. Google Play may also provide us with aggregated, non-identifying statistics (for example, total install counts, country-level breakdowns, crash rates, and star ratings). We cannot use this information to identify individual players. Please review the Google Privacy Policy for details on Google's practices.

6No Advertising, Analytics, or Third-Party Trackers

The Game contains no third-party advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no social or tracking SDKs. Nothing in the Game builds a profile of you or follows your activity across other apps or websites.

7Children's Privacy

The Game is family-friendly and is not directed at any particular age group. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it likewise collects no personal information from children. If you believe a child has somehow provided us personal information, please contact us and we will address it promptly.

8Data Security

Since the Game does not collect or transmit personal data, there is no personal data held by us that could be exposed in a breach. Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own operating-system security and any screen lock or encryption you have enabled.

9Your Choices and Rights

You are always in control of the only data the Game creates:

  • To reset your progress and preferences, clear the app's storage/data in your device settings.
  • To remove all locally stored game data, uninstall the Game.
  • You may block the Game's internet access at the operating-system level; the Game remains playable, though custom display fonts may fall back to a system font.

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. As explained above, we hold no such data about you, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase on your behalf.

10International Users

The Game can be played worldwide. Because it does not send personal data off your device to us, no international transfer of your personal data by the developer takes place. Any data handling by Google (Play Store, Google Fonts) is governed by Google's own policies and infrastructure.

11Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time, for example if the Game adds a new feature. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page and, where required, note the change in the app's store listing. Continued use of the Game after an update means you accept the revised policy.
